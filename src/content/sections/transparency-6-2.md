As mentioned before, transparency is the degree to which the processes producing a gestalt remain apparent in experience, on all three layers of the compression gradient, while the gestalt is still being experienced and used. This is not to be confused with phenomenal transparency, which is the invisibility of a model’s construction. Transparency is hard when ontological authority is high because a gestalt that other structures are built on top of is what the field is currently running on rather than something the field can perceive as an object. Reification is the “extreme” case of this: construction of a thing is not seen at all and is taken to be inherent to reality. Transparency is not preventing all structure; we still need that to survive and go about our day. It is preventing structure from becoming hidden so we can see through what other interpretations of input can offer. Transparency becomes practical then rather than theoretical because it offers us paths of freedom in perception we would not usually have due to branches in the load-bearing tree opening up to us.

Transparency is about redistributing precision in helpful ways. We can move precision in the load-bearing tree from the whole of the gestalt towards its constituent parts that make up its construction. This means the whole loses its high authority status without fully being removed from experience. This leads to ontological authority and organization of experience coming apart. A gestalt can keep organizing experience while still being held as transparent. For example, you can still see a dog and react to danger if it appears, but now the reaction to danger only appears depending on the current instance of dog and its parts, not every time a dog shows up. The gestalt of dog did not disappear since it still organizes certain parts like "4 legs" and "fur"; what went away is the over generalization that the original gestalt kept providing which was causing inaccurate modeling across all situations. When we start placing more weight on the parts making up the gestalt in the present moment instead of allowing it to hold top-down power, we start contracting less. To take a deeper example, "I am unsafe" is a gestalt that if given enough ontological authority, can produce lots of suffering in every moment due to producing unhelpful targets and contraction. Given low authority, we can see its parts like a fast heart beat, which allows us to redirect precision in a way that moves from the top-down label of unsafe to simply "oh this is just a fast heart beat." Bear in mind, simply moving precision to constructing parts is sometimes not enough since deep latched contraction in the body can hold patterns that feed back into the original gestalt's confirming evidence.

Once a gestalt stops being treated as a static structure after enough practice, it still integrates input at the same cost, if not less. This is because the parts now prune the load-bearing tree instead of the whole gestalt, so you keep the cheap integration while losing authority. Importantly, the integration cost staying the same or reducing occurs after enough practice; when first trying to hold the gestalt as transparent, it can be expensive to compute the individual parts in each moment, and can fail when you don't have enough slack like when tired. We might retreat back into old patterns that are a product of the old gestalt organizing experience. So, transparency is not about simply flipping a switch; it is an active training process done until parts can be seen in detail and be taken as paths in the load-bearing tree.

If we look back on the constructive process and its inverse, it is clear transparency is the practical version of de-chunking a gestalt into relations and tracing them to conditions. If we want to use this inverse process successfully, reification must drop so the internal workings can start to be seen through rather than as invisible. This leads to the deconstruction process leaving more branches open in the load-bearing tree due to components becoming separately addressable, so each one suggests paths the original gestalt left out of reach.

Crucially when trying to achieve high transparency, this can be difficult due to perspective limitations. A high authority gestalt is what the field is running on for structure, so naturally trying to see through it will be like trying to see your own eyes. Transparency then in some cases becomes an achievement of getting around perspectival limitations. Holding alternative organizations of experience costs resource capacity like attention, which means we need a sufficient level of slack. Transparency can fail under conditions like fatigue and threat due to this if it is not conditioned in the field enough.

This is because transparency works by changing whether the field contracts and commits to a certain configuration at all. Transparency does not simply add more capacity, but that can be a side effect. To understand this, "having slack" can mean two different things: the spare capacity you have to spend to get out of a stuck state, and the condition in which whether the field latches onto a state in the first place. Factors like unclenching and good sleep raise the former, and transparency and equanimity helps the ladder. A fully rested person with good health can still easily latch onto stuck states which lie in deep attractor basins, like holding onto critique that someone gave them and getting upset about it. On the other hand, someone with high equanimity and can see through the construction of gestalts can let the critique drop quickly even if tired or has poor external circumstances. This is why releasing contraction, both bodily and mentally, doesn't simply fade back into the background felt tone like a new material item would– what was removed was capacity uselessly spent that can now be spent elsewhere. This is also why rest and insight are not the same– someone can take a month long break from a situation, come back, and still latch back onto the same field configuration just as easily as before it they become triggered. You also can't do the opposite: have the insight but be depleted in terms of capacity and expect for the change to stay active. Someone with high equanimity might reach extreme exhaustion and not be able to access that skill, and fall back into latched patterns if they are still kept in the system somewhere. Again, the important skill here is equanimity, which allows competing branches in the load-bearing tree to stay open so passing configurations do not become latched again.

Take for example trying to de-chunk the parts of high anxiety when in a deep attractor basin. You can know the “correct” move in this case, but seeing it as transparent is too costly from the system’s perspective. This also tells us why depth of priors and gestalts predict difficulty to see as transparent: trunk-level structure resists change because almost everything is built on top. Another problem of gaining transparency is context-sensitivity in which ontological authority can drop in one context while staying high in another, which is one reason why you need multiple passes of the refinement loop. Like with high anxiety states, one context can make the state authoritative enough that transparency does not occur, while in another the same components separate easily.

Another important aspect about cultivating transparency deals with the depth of the gestalt you are attempting to recognize as transparent instead of authoritative. We can view experience across a ladder of perspectives, where each step down reveals a layer of construction that is progressively harder to see through. For instance if we take the high level anxiety state, at the top the gestalt might be something like “these sensations are scary” and seeing that as transparent just means deconstructing the sensations themselves. Farther below that sits something like “these sensations are happening to me” which is a threat prior that made the interpretation of sensations feel highly authoritative in the first place. Making this gestalt transparent changes every situation in which similar sensations occur, not just this one. We can go all the way down the ladder until we hit something like thing-ness itself or innate priors. Each level is harder to see through as a construction because more is built on it which makes updating proportionally costly. Depth of the gestalt being targeted predicts both the difficulty and the generality of the change through transparency. Whether repeated practice at high levels loosens deeper structures through propagation or simply builds the clarity and slack needed for direct low-level work is unclear; either way, working with high-level structure matters.

When it comes to somatics, as stated earlier, latched contraction associated with the trunk of the load-bearing tree is high ontological authority held in the body. Because of this, epistemic transparency without bodily tension release allows the reified gestalt to propagate back through the system, as interoceptive evidence remains structured by the very thing being deconstructed. This means holding transparency and releasing haptic contraction are processes that end up with similar results, since both lower the precision and embodied reinforcement keeping a prior stable. However, this mechanism only applies to contraction that has not been held long. If contraction has been held long enough in the body as to make itself deep, like through habitual repetition of suppression, the range of patterns the mind-body system can produce and manage becomes narrowed and stays that way. At that point, releasing the contraction and restoring the patterns that were closed off becomes two different processes, not just one. Meaning, releasing deep, latched contraction in one instance that has been in the system for a long time (exact time unknown) does not mean you will automatically regain the patterns that were lost due to that stuck contraction. In speculation, deep haptic contraction sits in a basin and needs extra resources, like energy, supplied from the outside to escape and be released. This is why interventions like psychedelics, heat, and sustained attention are ultimately achieving the same thing and can result in lasting change. An analogous metaphor here is like a joint stuck in a cast: short term you remove the cast and the joint moves again, long enough time spent in the cast and the literal tissue of the body changes, so producing the range of the joint again means having to use it and practice until it becomes viable. This implies that a collapsed load-bearing tree due to deep, latched haptic contraction produces reachability constraints to other field configurations. So, you might be able to “see” through a deep prior that is “stuck,” but other field configurations will be unreachable because the body cannot supply the state the other configuration needs. This also increases the importance of slack for deep change because we need to inch ourselves closer to the states we want to be in that were once unreachable through repetition in small amounts until the body can handle them. Equanimity then matters even more as its own skill, since holding intensity without the field collapsing prevents deep contraction from forming in the first place, while transparency is what expands possibilities that were once collapsed.

Expanding on the importance of sensory clarity, it seems as though sensory clarity is transparency viewed through an embodied lens: de-chunking a sensation gestalt by resolving it into changing lower-level sensations. This is why sensory clarity reduces suffering according to the energetic triad, because once a sensation stops functioning as an authoritative structure, less of the field has to be organized around it, so the ongoing cost of integrating input lessens and the negative felt tone decreases.

And in general this is why transparency reduces suffering. The suffering loop as laid out in section 5.2 starts with first order hurt appearing as a gestalt that carries high authority which organizes the field around it, and its subsequent branches like beliefs that the hurt should not be happening. This then leaves control, craving or aversion, as the only available action policy to be enacted to remove that target error. Once the construction of the gestalt is known, less of the field has to be organized around it, so the ongoing integration cost of input falls, and each part of the gestalt branches out separately in the load-bearing tree, so there are more action policies available. This mechanism can also apply to memories, since recalling one reinstates its relevant field configuration and somatic pattern (contingent on context like slack, mood, and environment). When the memory's gestalts hold high authority, the contraction latches which then confirms the prediction that produced it. When the contraction is not reinstated, the configuration still reconstructs and sensations may arise, like a fast heartbeat, but they are not tightly bound to being defended against, so the loop that would reinforce the relevant field configuration and contraction does not run.

Making transparency of structure obvious can make certain field configurations more reachable by opening paths the constructed gestalt closed. When a gestalt the configuration depended on loses enough ontological authority, other contending branches in the load-bearing tree stay active as available paths rather than being pruned. This then weakens the attractor basin holding the current field configuration as being stable by reducing depth and width. The conditions for exiting the basin become easier to meet due to barriers being dropped and more options being open, so the asymmetry between entering and exiting the state is reduced. Transparency does not completely remove the basin though since a configuration is also instantiated by other things like bodily states, environment, and attention which can all keep reinstating it.

Importantly when training transparency, it should be made clear that the level of the thing you are targeting to recognize as being transparent matters a great deal. Meaning, the hierarchical position of the structure you are attempting to see the construction of determines if it will actually have a meaningful impact or not. If we decide to make gestalts transparent that sit on the same level in the load-bearing tree, we would end up working towards little. Let’s say the gestalt of “this anxiety is dangerous” is seen as constructed and its inner workings are made obvious which reduces its ontological authority status; no longer will it organize the field to its liking. However, there’s a high chance that the gestalt of “this frustration is unbearable” will simply “take over” that spot because they lie on very similar levels in the load-bearing tree. By remaining shallow, they both do not have a deep organizational impact on experience, so the chance transparency effects dependent structure that lies on top of those gestalts is low. Now, that is assuming the transparency insights simply stay at those high levels, but because transparency targets their construction, it is not uncommon for deeper changes to propagate deeper in the load-bearing tree; these types of changes however will take longer to occur rather than just the shallow gestalt becoming transparent. If we spend enough time holding transparency around these high-level gestalts, there is a chance the insights will propagate downwards and reach lower-level gestalts like “there are solid sensations.” However, if this happens, and if high-level gestalts are simply “replaced” when they no longer have high ontological authority, then it would make sense to target low-level gestalts that have a much more profound impact on organizing the field. This way, we can make bigger changes faster through targeting generative structures which has the potential to allow us to move between states with more freedom. Intuitively, the system would need to be holding a sufficient amount of slack here for these changes to be optimal instead of destabilizing, which is why moving downwards at a steady pace can be helpful instead of simply only targeting the lowest level thing we can.

It seems as though “thing-ness” is the deepest gestalt we can practically target to hold as transparent within our system without becoming completely destabilized. Crucially, thing-ness is simply a construction like all other gestalts, meaning its parts and interactions matter in the way we target it to hold it as transparent. Its construction seems to be made up of processes like input being segmented into bounded units, those units cohering as single things, and their persistence across a seemingly continuous temporal slice. If we can lower the ontological authority of thing-ness, we have a chance at reducing a massive amount of suffering due to almost every other gestalt depending on it, so the core action policy of craving and aversion loses the structure it was using as a substrate and that policy stops being the only available response.

In terms of the energetic triad, holding thing-ness as transparent means the field no longer has to hold it as a high authority structure, so the ongoing cost of integrating input falls, and an update pertaining to any particular gestalt that depends on thing-ness propagates less through the system, so negative differences in felt tone shrink. This also changes what resonance and friction are signals of, since they now keep track of integrating input into a field with more available branches which makes the surprise of resonance or friction propagates through less structure in the system so the difference in experience is smaller. In terms of the baseline of experience, the background felt tone rises because less resources go to holding reified things, and because fewer of the load-bearing tree’s targets are ones that the world cannot fulfill, like permanence, so there is less error and hurt in experience. A situation is then held in the body as something with moving parts rather than as one solid thing that has to be enacted, which is why the felt shift becomes available more often due to more pathways being opened up by differentiating the solid thing.

(Credit to Roger Thisdell’s A Very Streamlined Enlightenment Protocol for first introducing me to the concept of targeting low-level priors)

To be clear, holding transparency around gestalts does not mean the way you model things is now more truthful or corresponds with reality. Something can be seen as constructed yet still be wrong, which is why open loops of refinement are necessary. However, transparency does make the refinement loop easier as it clears barriers that would normally prevent updating, like defense mechanisms. As laid out in the suffering loop section, epistemic suffering is when we hold craving or aversion around epistemic models or concepts and habitually contract around them so they do not update. Seeing a model as constructed out of conditions rather than as reified reality means input that would usually create friction is no longer a threat that needs to be avoided. Objectification is a step in the refinement loop, since we have to clearly isolate the thing first to inspect it, and high ontological authority blocks this by making the thing the structure itself instead of an object. Once we can successfully inspect the parts a gestalt depends on, we can run the loop on those which means resonance and friction can pertain to deeper, more fundamental priors which means more things on top get updated. This helps undercut the self-maintenance problem of gestalts confirming themselves through regeneration of contraction. This is analogous to the sensory clarity skill in meditation: seeing conditions of a high authority gestalt is like seeing how bodily sensations are made up, through input becoming more differentiated over time. This is why blurriness, the opposite of sensory clarity and transparency causes suffering: it regenerates the conditions of the substrate giving way to the suffering in the first place.

As for failure modes like depersonalization and derealization, the conditions leading to these states are complex and I’m not sure if there is any one algorithmic formula to understand how they work, but what is obvious is that these types of states are not about holding transparency, but about over-reifying things. The structures organizing experience are still given high authority, there is just a disconnection between certain ones that matter to the system like “real-ness” and “self-hood” through the loss of interoceptive evidence. I believe states like DP/DR are configurations we enter into when our system does not hold enough slack to handle the way it is organizing experience. This is why releasing contraction and “surrendering” to the state can be helpful: it stops the system from spending resources on the policy that the state be different, which is the contraction keeping the state up in the first place.

Lastly, developing transparency should not lead to paralysis. A system holding wise and genuine transparency can still compress things and commit to singular interpretations. Nothing about recognizing construction of gestalts should require holding every alternative branch open, which would be costly and useless. The difference is that the system can update when context changes, rather than being stuck with whatever field configuration it started with. Acting firmly while holding a gestalt with low ontological authority are not in tension, since compression is what makes action possible and transparency makes updating possible. Think of someone who holds a model so close and tightly that nothing can penetrate it, like a flat-earther. Then action may be firm but in no way can they enter the loop of refinement oriented towards truth-making which would allow them to see the belief as transparent. This is often what happens as we age and continue to hold rigid models about how things work.

This points to what the important target is in this framework: ontological authority is not the problem itself, it is authority that the system cannot see clearly and drop. Committing to a gestalt fully while keeping its authority low is what kids do when they play, what adults do when thinking within specific frames, and what phenomenological practices can do when propagating feelings of safety down into the mind-body system. All of these moves depend on the system’s ability to treat a thing as given, then stop on command without contraction. The core question then becomes what to grant authority to and when. This is how sanity and wisdom come to matter.
