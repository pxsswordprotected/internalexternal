Through this framework, is all qualia, or phenomenological sensations , “phantom predictions” of perception, generated through the field or structure , hence why in cessation this goes to 0? This would also fit in with traditional phantom sensations like a made up phone buzz in ur pocket

**The generative part is solid, and you have better evidence than the phone buzz.** Charles Bonnet syndrome is the killer case: people who have lost their sight entirely get vivid, detailed, structured visual experience. No input at all, full visual qualia. Sensory deprivation produces the same thing across modalities. Phantom limb gives you pain in a limb that does not exist. These show generation is *sufficient* on its own, which is the strongest form of your claim.

**Where to land it instead.** The useful consequence isn't cessation, it's real-making. If experience is generated and generation is shaped by the field, then changing the field changes **what appears**, not just how you feel about what appears. Phantom sensations are your proof of concept for that, because they show the field can produce a complete appearance with nothing outside supporting it. That's the claim worth making, and it's one your framework is unusually well set up to defend.
